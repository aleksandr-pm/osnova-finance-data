DTC Reports Q3 2026 Revenue of AED 735.5 Million

■ Reported revenue increased 25.7% YoY in Q3 2026 to AED 735.5 million, reflecting the consolidation of National Taxi from 8 July 2026, while reported EBITDA increased 10.0% to AED 166.5 million. ■ DTC standalone taxi and limousine trip volumes improved progressively through Q3 2026, with year-on-year (YoY) decline narrowing from 7.8% in July to 1.9% in September. ■ Reported net profit was AED 57.1 million in Q3 2026, down 25.3% reflecting lower underlying profitability and higher financing costs in the period. ■ ~30% of National Taxi integration completed, including key controls and operational alignment, with AED 3.9 million of synergies realised in Q3, ahead of initial expectations. ■ DTC continued to advance its five-year strategy, with 450 of 600 new Dubai taxi plates operational by quarter-end, further expansion of the Bolt network, commissioning of an ultra-fast EV charging hub, and continued rollout of autonomous vehicles in partnership with Apollo Go. ■ Post period end, five-year renewal of DTC’s strategic agreement with Hala, through the RTA-Careem joint venture, deepening one of Dubai’s longest-running e-hailing partnerships.
